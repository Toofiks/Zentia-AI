Welcome to Zentia

Let's deploy your first AI Sales Agent. Follow these steps to automate your top-of-funnel.

1 Get a Bot Token

Open @BotFather in Telegram, send /newbot, and copy the HTTP API Token. This links your AI to Telegram.

2 Add API Key

Go to the Settings tab (bottom left) and insert your OpenRouter or Gemini API Key. This is the brain of your bot.

3 Create Your Agent

Click the button below to open the agent builder. Fill in the token and tell the AI what its goal is!
