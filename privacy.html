Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2026

1. Introduction

At Zentia AI, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our platform.

2. Data We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide our service:

  • Email address and basic profile info via Supabase Auth.
  • Telegram Bot tokens provided by you to run your agents.
  • Messages processed by your bots (stored securely for your inbox).
  • Custom API keys (stored encrypted in your user metadata).

3. How We Use Data

Your data is used strictly for:

  • Authenticating your access to the dashboard.
  • Operating your AI agents on Telegram.
  • Generating analytics for your account.
  • Processing subscription payments via Stripe.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with necessary service providers:

  • Supabase: Database and Authentication.
  • Stripe: Payment processing.
  • OpenRouter/Google: To process AI requests (only the content of messages is sent).

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption for your API keys and secure protocols for all communication. Your Telegram tokens are used only to interface with the Telegram API.

6. Your Rights

You can delete your account and all associated agents at any time through the dashboard. This will purge your data from our active systems.

7. Contact

If you have questions, please contact us at privacy@zentia-ai.com.
